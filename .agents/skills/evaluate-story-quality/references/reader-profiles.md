# Reader profiles

Version: `reader-profiles-v1`

Age changes developmental expectations. Reading mode independently changes
delivery and decoding expectations. Do not infer ability from age alone.

## Ages 3–5

- Prefer immediate, concrete goals and visible cause/effect.
- Expect strong support for chronology, motives, and required inference.
- Favor pattern, recurrence, and participation with meaningful variation.
- Prefer fewer simultaneous characters, locations, hidden motives, or time
  shifts.
- Expect short conversational turns and orally fluent phrasing.
- Allow rich words when context or repetition supports them.
- Expect readable emotional changes and reassuring closure appropriate to the
  story's intensity.
- A quiet story may succeed without high suspense, but still needs forward
  movement and a consequential child action.

## Ages 6–7

- Allow somewhat longer causal chains and delayed consequences.
- Expect clear motivation with modest supported inference.
- Allow more varied syntax and richer vocabulary in read-aloud modes.
- For independent reading, prioritize cohesion and manageable decoding load.
- Expect attempts to change the situation rather than merely repeat it.

## Ages 8–10

- Allow layered causal chains, delayed payoff, and competing perspectives.
- Expect greater capacity for supported inference and emotional ambiguity.
- Allow richer vocabulary and more complex syntax in read-aloud mode.
- Expect meaningful agency, changed attempts, and an earned payoff.
- Permit interpretive space after plot closure, but not missing causal facts.

## Reading modes

### `parent_read_aloud`

Prioritize oral flow, listening comprehension, supported rich vocabulary,
dialogue clarity, and opportunities for shared prediction or discussion. Do not
penalize words solely because a child may not decode them independently.

### `co_read`

Apply read-aloud expectations and also look for approachable participation
lines, repeated language, and places where the child can join without losing
the narrative.

### `independent_developing`

Prioritize decoding load, sentence cohesion, explicit referents, controlled
text density, and strong support for inference. Rich language remains welcome
when context makes it recoverable.

### `independent_confident`

Allow greater syntactic and vocabulary complexity while requiring coherent
causal structure, clear knowledge states, and supported inference.
