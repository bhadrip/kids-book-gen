## Citation cautions

- Publisher and practitioner sources support production conventions, not developmental claims.
- Preprints on AI image generation describe rapidly changing technical methods and should be revisited before implementation decisions.
- Findings from preschool studies should not be presented as validated thresholds for ages 7–10.
- A bibliography entry does not imply that the project may copy or train on the work's full text or images.
