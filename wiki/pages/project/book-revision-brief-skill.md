# Book revision brief skill

[`build-book-revision-brief`](../../../.agents/skills/build-book-revision-brief/SKILL.md)
consolidates parent feedback and evaluator findings into a dependency-aware
repair plan. It assigns one primary artifact owner to every root issue, orders
repairs upstream-first, preserves approved strengths, prevents unnecessary
sibling regeneration, and defines measurable closure evidence and evaluator
reruns.

The brief is advisory and version-aware. It does not silently modify an approved
story, plan, illustration, approval state, or final book.
