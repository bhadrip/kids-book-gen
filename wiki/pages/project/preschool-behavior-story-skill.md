# Preschool behavior story skill

The project-local `preschool-behavior-story` skill creates emotionally engaging
picture-book stories for ages 3–5 in which a desired behavior emerges through
character purpose, emotion, agency, and natural consequence.

The workflow separates behavior intent, story DNA, spread planning, critique,
manuscript drafting, and production handoff. It requires one primary dramatic
question and checks every spread for necessity so secondary behaviors do not
become a new lesson after the climax.
