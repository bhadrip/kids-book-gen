## Overall observation

The planning artifacts describe a coherent progression for Milo and consistently
protect against shame, coercion, forced eating, adult surveillance, and
moralized clean-plate praise. The noted inconsistencies occur primarily in the
translation from those approved intentions into visible performance on the
generated pages.

The illustrations frequently use a generally cheerful expression. That keeps
the book warm and safe, but makes some neighboring emotional states less
distinct than the plan requires.

## Noted inconsistencies

### Spreads 1–2: Awareness is too subtle

- **Planned transition:** comfortable habit → first hint of awareness.
- **Expected evidence:** Milo visibly notices the remaining bites through a
  glance, pause, or change in attention.
- **Observed evidence:** Milo appears comfortable at the meal. The following
  spread emphasizes the isolated plate, but Milo's internal change is difficult
  to distinguish.
- **Result:** `could_be_clearer`.
- **Revision suggestion:** preserve the warm, nonjudgmental meal and make Milo's
  backward glance or pause more readable.
- **Preserve:** no shame, scolding, guilt, or implication that Milo is careless.

### Spreads 4–5: Reflection resolves too quickly

- **Planned transition:** reflection → appreciation → readiness.
- **Expected evidence:** attentive focus, gentle hands, and a quiet appreciative
  expression before Milo reaches his solution.
- **Observed evidence:** the food's journey is visualized clearly and Milo stays
  positive, but the internal recognition beat moves quickly into readiness.
- **Result:** `could_be_clearer`.
- **Revision suggestion:** preserve the food-journey panorama and give Milo one
  quieter gesture or expression of recognition before the solution beat.
- **Preserve:** appreciation without guilt or an obligation to finish eating.

### Spread 6: Confidence reads as triumph

- **Planned transition:** readiness → self-directed confidence.
- **Expected evidence:** Milo proposes his own idea with pleased, thoughtful
  confidence and without a triumphant or moralizing pose.
- **Observed evidence:** Milo raises one finger enthusiastically while his family
  watches. His agency is clear, but the performance is more triumphant than the
  approved plan requests.
- **Result:** `could_be_clearer`.
- **Revision suggestion:** keep Milo as the source of the idea while softening
  the pose into calm, pleased confidence.
- **Preserve:** Milo's ownership of the solution and the family's supportive
  attention.

### Spread 9: Relief is not distinct

- **Planned transition:** comfortably full → relieved through practical care.
- **Expected evidence:** Milo visibly relaxes when he understands that the
  remaining food can be saved without pressure.
- **Observed evidence:** collaboration and family warmth are visible, but Milo's
  relief is not distinct from the cheerful expressions on adjacent pages.
- **Result:** `could_be_clearer`.
- **Revision suggestion:** preserve the cooperative storage action and show a
  small release of tension, such as relaxed shoulders when the container closes.
- **Preserve:** Milo participates meaningfully; the family helps without blame or
  taking over.

### Spread 11: Patient persistence is mostly inferred

- **Planned transition:** imperfect attempts → patient persistence and
  self-compassion.
- **Expected evidence:** uncertainty visibly settles into a calm willingness to
  notice, adjust, and try again.
- **Observed evidence:** the montage shows surprise, uncertainty, and success,
  but the text summarizes learning and the patient emotional bridge is mostly
  inferred.
- **Result:** `could_be_clearer`.
- **Revision suggestion:** preserve the varied attempts and add a calm
  trying-again expression or another visual link between uncertainty and later
  confidence.
- **Preserve:** mistakes remain normal practice rather than failure.

## Learning recorded

A consistent `EmotionalArc`, `SpreadMap`, and `BookPlan` do not guarantee that
the final images will visibly distinguish each planned transition. Emotional
fidelity therefore needs to be evaluated after page generation, when the full
illustrated sequence exists, and before final book approval so any accepted
finding can use localized page revision.
