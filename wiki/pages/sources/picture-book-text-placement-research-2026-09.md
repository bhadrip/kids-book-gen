# Picture-book text-placement research, September 2026

This source synthesis supports the project's page-specific placement method for
ages 3–5. The preserved research memo and bibliography are in
[`wiki/raw/page-specific-picture-book-text-placement-research-2026-09-09.md`](../../raw/page-specific-picture-book-text-placement-research-2026-09-09.md).

## Source strength

The evidence base combines:

- first-person professional practice from picture-book illustrators, designers,
  and art-direction coverage;
- official printer specifications from KDP and IngramSpark;
- W3C and DAISY fixed-layout and accessibility guidance;
- peer-reviewed eye-tracking and comprehension research.

The professional accounts consistently support composing real text with art at
the rough stage, preserving room for type, checking the gutter, and allowing
the final designer to refine placement. The research supports treating pictures
as narrative information that children coordinate with spoken text. Official
production and accessibility sources supply measurable constraints but do not
define a complete preschool print-layout standard.

## Durable conclusion

A nominal text-safe corner is an input to composition, not a validation result.
Final placement must be chosen from page-specific candidates on the actual
composed illustration. Candidates must pass production geometry, capacity,
local contrast, protected-subject, reading-order, and output-integrity gates.
The remaining alternatives may be scored automatically, but emotionally and
narratively sensitive decisions require human review.

See [Page-specific picture-book text placement](../concepts/page-specific-picture-book-text-placement.md)
for the project method and proposed artifact contract.

## Limits and uncertainty

No universal publishing rule was found for safe corners, preschool point size,
saliency thresholds, clearance distances, or permissible subject overlap.
WCAG is normative for applicable digital content, not a complete print standard.
Proposed numerical scoring weights, complexity thresholds, and treatment costs
must be calibrated with professional review and physical proofs.
